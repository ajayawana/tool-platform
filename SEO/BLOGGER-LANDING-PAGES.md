### Body

# DSCR Calculator

Use the free Ozarbox DSCR Calculator to calculate your debt service coverage ratio from net operating income and annual debt service.

## DSCR formula

**DSCR = Net Operating Income ÷ Annual Debt Service**

For example, if net operating income is 120,000 and annual debt service is 80,000, DSCR is 1.50x.

## What DSCR means

DSCR measures how much operating income is available to cover scheduled debt service. A higher ratio means more operating income relative to the stated debt service, but lenders may use their own definitions, adjustments and minimum thresholds.

## Assumptions

Use figures from the same reporting period. Confirm whether your lender defines NOI, debt service, add-backs and other adjustments differently.

## 2. Freelancer Hourly Rate Calculator

**Blogger title:** Freelancer Hourly Rate Calculator: Find a Sustainable Rate

**Search description:** Estimate a freelancer hourly rate from target income, expenses, working hours and billable utilisation. Free calculator with formula and assumptions.

**Suggested URL slug:** `freelancer-hourly-rate-calculator`

### Body

# Freelancer Hourly Rate Calculator

Your hourly rate has to cover more than the hours you spend doing client work. Use this calculator to estimate a sustainable rate from your income target, business expenses, available working hours and billable utilisation.

## Basic idea

A simple planning model is:

**Required revenue = target income + business expenses**

**Hourly rate = required revenue ÷ billable hours**

Billable hours are lower than total working hours because freelancers also spend time on sales, administration, learning, proposals and unpaid gaps between projects.

## Example

If your target annual income is 60,000, expenses are 12,000, annual working hours are 2,000 and billable utilisation is 60%, the calculation uses 1,200 billable hours. The resulting planning rate is 60 per hour before any additional tax or profit-buffer assumptions.

## Important assumption

This is a planning calculator, not a tax calculator. Your actual tax, social contributions, GST/VAT obligations and deductible expenses depend on your country and circumstances.

Calculate a target consulting day rate from annual income, business costs and realistic billable days.

**Search description:** Calculate the gross amount required to receive a target net amount after an assumed tax rate. Includes formula and example.

### Body

# Tax Gross-Up Calculator

If you need a target net amount after an assumed withholding or tax rate, a gross-up calculation estimates the gross amount required.

**Gross amount = target net amount ÷ (1 − tax rate)**

For example, at a 25% assumed rate, receiving 5,000 net would require a gross amount of about 6,666.67 under this simplified model.

Actual withholding and tax rules vary by jurisdiction and payment type.

### Body

# VAT Inclusive & Exclusive Calculator

When a quoted price already includes VAT, you can reverse the VAT to estimate the net price and VAT component.

**Net price = VAT-inclusive price ÷ (1 + VAT rate)**

**VAT amount = VAT-inclusive price − net price**

Always use the VAT rate that applies to the transaction and jurisdiction.
